The problem today

Three journeys. Rarely in step.

Every clinic day, three separate journeys unfold around the same appointment — each running on its own, each carrying its own weight.

Patient

The long wait

Books an appointment, then waits — away from work and family, never quite sure when the turn will come.

  • Unclear token status
  • Hours in waiting rooms
  • Forgotten follow-ups

…a journey with a gap in the middle

Doctor

The full day

A packed OPD, a queue to keep fair, notes to write — and every patient's next visit to remember.

  • Back-to-back slots
  • Manual queue calls
  • Follow-up blind spots

…a journey with a gap in the middle

Hospital

The busy desk

The front desk juggles calls, walk-ins, and tokens while every counter asks for the same information again.

  • Phone-only booking
  • Paper tokens
  • Fragmented view

…a journey with a gap in the middle
